# Amdavad Oven — Investor & Operating Partner Brief
**Founder:** Saharsh Srivastava  
**Proposed location:** Naranpura / Navrangpura, Ahmedabad, Gujarat  
**Concept:** Premium Italian dining, artisan pizza and specialty café  
**Planning concept:** Approximately 100 seats and 4,500 sq. ft. flagship; compact in-house delivery kitchen; vegetarian and non-vegetarian menu; Italian foundations with selected Gujarati/Indian inspirations; immersive projection and spatial-audio dining elements as optional phased capex.

## Executive summary
Amdavad Oven is a proposed premium dining brand for Ahmedabad. The concept combines artisan pizza and Italian food with a high-quality café experience and a distinctive, immersive flagship environment. The founder proposes to lead brand strategy, marketing, customer acquisition, partnerships and technology. A professional chef/operator and experienced hospitality team would lead food quality, kitchen operations, compliance, procurement and service.

## The opportunity to validate
- Demand for premium artisan pizza and Italian dining in the selected Ahmedabad catchment.
- Willingness to pay for food quality, hospitality and a differentiated dine-in experience.
- Repeat purchase, delivery economics, average order value and contribution margin.
These are hypotheses. They must be validated through competitor mapping, customer interviews, pop-ups or a pilot kitchen before committing to a large lease or fit-out.

## Proposed format
- Catchment: Naranpura / Navrangpura, subject to site survey, rent and access.
- Size: approximately 4,500 sq. ft.; 100 seats (concept assumptions, not a secured property).
- Menu: vegetarian and non-vegetarian artisan pizzas, pasta, selected Italian mains, desserts, specialty coffee and non-alcoholic beverages.
- Kitchen: compact in-house production/delivery capability integrated with dine-in.
- Experience: projection/spatial audio only after core kitchen, safety, acoustics and unit economics are proven.

## Founder contribution and operating model
Founder cash contribution: currently ₹0. Founder contribution is proposed as concept development, brand, marketing, technology, customer acquisition and partnerships. The business therefore requires an investor and/or operating partner. An experienced hospitality operator and executive chef are essential hires/partners, not optional assumptions.

## Capital requirement
A definitive capital ask is **not yet established**. The earlier ₹10 crore figure is a staged long-term fundraising ambition, not a verified opening budget or current offer. The 4,500 sq. ft. flagship needs a site-specific project report covering deposit, rent, civil/interior works, kitchen equipment, HVAC/fire systems, licenses, pre-opening payroll, inventory, launch marketing and at least 6–9 months working capital. Obtain 3 vendor quotations and a property term sheet before fixing the amount.
 
## Revenue model and financial diligence
Revenue sources: dine-in, takeaway, direct delivery, catering/events and packaged products only if separately validated. No sales, margins, customer counts or profit forecasts are represented as achieved. Build a bottom-up model using:
1. seat count × table turns × occupancy × average realized bill;
2. delivery orders × average order value;
3. food cost, packaging, platform commissions, payroll, rent, utilities, maintenance, marketing, taxes and wastage.
Break-even, EBITDA, payback and investor returns should be calculated only after real quotations, menu costing and pilot data.

## Proposed funding structure (to negotiate)
Seeking discussion with a strategic investor, hospitality operator or kitchen partner. Possible structures include equity, convertible instruments or a joint venture, subject to valuation, legal/tax review and definitive agreements. No equity percentage or return is promised in this brief.

## Milestones
1. 2–4 weeks: catchment and competitor study; customer validation; chef/operator shortlist.
2. 3–6 weeks: menu prototypes, recipe costing, supplier quotes and pilot/pop-up.
3. 2–4 weeks: site shortlist, lease due diligence, architectural and equipment estimates.
4. Investment committee: verified DPR, cap table, cash-flow model, risk register and funding terms.
5. Only after funding and approvals: fit-out, hiring, trial service and launch.

## Key risks and mitigations
- High capex / lease risk: stage the project; validate with pop-up or delivery pilot first.
- Food consistency: appoint an experienced chef and standardize recipes, yield and QA.
- Demand risk: paid pilot and cohort/retention tracking before full build-out.
- Compliance: FSSAI, local municipal permissions, fire and building safety, labour, GST and other applicable approvals before opening.
- Founder execution gap: hire experienced hospitality operations leadership and agree clear governance.

## What is requested from a prospective partner
A meeting to evaluate (a) investment interest, (b) operating-partner capability, (c) kitchen/pilot partnership, and (d) support in preparing a bankable detailed project report. The proposal is non-binding and subject to diligence.
